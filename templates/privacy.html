Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

1. Data We Collect

Spendly collects only what it needs to work as an expense tracker. This falls into two categories:

  • Account details — the username and email address you provide at registration, along with a securely hashed version of your password.
  • Expense records — the amount, category, date, and description of each expense you choose to log.

We do not ask for and never store bank account numbers, card details, or credentials for any financial institution. Spendly does not connect to your bank.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide the service back to you — nothing more. Specifically, to authenticate you when you sign in, to display your expense list, and to generate the category breakdowns, monthly summaries, and date-range reports you request.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers, and we do not use your expense records to build advertising profiles or train models.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense records are stored in the application's database. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed before being written, so they cannot be read back by anyone, including us.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may delete individual expense records at any time from within the application, and deleting your account removes the expense records associated with it.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly keeps external dependencies to a minimum. The only third party service used by these pages is Google Fonts, which serves the typefaces used across the site. When a page loads, your browser requests those font files directly from Google, which may involve your IP address being visible to them.

We do not use analytics trackers, advertising pixels, or social media embeds. No third party has access to your expense data.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, want to know what data we hold about you, or would like your account and records deleted, get in touch at privacy@spendly.app and we will respond as soon as we can.

We may update this policy as the service develops. When we do, the "Last updated" date above will change.
